About

I'm interested in how AI can augment human capabilities—particularly in learning, language, and creative work.

Current Focus

Building multi-agent systems that can serve as specialized assistants. The Claude Agents project explores:

  • Prompt engineering for specific domains and tasks
  • Modular agent architecture with shared "stems" and specialized extensions
  • Systematic evaluation frameworks for AI behavior
  • Language learning applications with regional dialect focus

The Language Lab

The Language Lab is an experiment in dialect-specific language coaching. Standard language learning often focuses on a "neutral" form that doesn't match how people actually speak in specific regions.

Each coach specializes in a regional variety:

  • Costa Rican Spanish: Voseo, tico expressions, pura vida culture
  • Kyoto Japanese: Kansai dialect recognition, はる forms, cultural nuance
  • Québécois French: Ear training for the distinct accent and vocabulary
