How It Works

1. Bring Your API Key

You'll need an Anthropic API key to use the coaches. Your key is stored locally in your browser and only sent directly to Anthropic's API.

2. Chat Naturally

Start a conversation with your coach. They'll assess your level through natural dialogue and adjust their teaching to match your needs.

3. Learn in Context

Each coach teaches language with cultural context—not just grammar rules, but when and why to use expressions.

4. Give Feedback

Your feedback helps improve the coaches. Sessions can be exported for analysis and evaluation.
